# Community code of conduct

Help make Lumi and its docs a welcoming place to ask questions, share work, and disagree constructively. This applies to issues, pull requests, and other project-managed community spaces.

- Treat people with respect regardless of background, identity, experience, or employer.
- Critique ideas and code with specific, useful feedback.
- Respect privacy; do not share personal or confidential information without permission.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized attention, and personal attacks.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid spam or repeated unwanted contact.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct concerns, contact Nieve through [Nieve’s contact page](https://www.nieveconsulting.com/contact) and request a private conversation with project maintainers. Do not publish private evidence in an issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation. Responses should account for severity, context, and repeated behavior. Reports will be handled as privately as practical; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.
